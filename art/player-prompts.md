# 玩家立绘提示词

内置 imagegen 生成，男女各3张，原创成年侠客形象，透明背景。所有立绘仅用于外观，不绑定武学或属性。

## 青衫剑客 · 男

原图：`assets/players/male-qing.png`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a selectable player character full-body standing illustration for a Chinese wuxia roguelike card game, ONE adult original protagonist only.
Style/medium: premium hand-painted Chinese ink-wash meets precise gongbi character concept art, realistic adult anatomy, expressive original face, textured brushwork and cloth. Same art direction as a refined wuxia bestiary: muted parchment ochre, pine green, charcoal, costume-specific accent color, finely painted fabric, clean readable silhouette. Painterly, not 3D or photography.
Composition/framing: portrait canvas, FULL BODY from all hair to both boots, entire weapon visible, one centered figure occupies about 85 percent height with clear padding, three-quarter pose subtly facing RIGHT because the player stands on the LEFT of the battle screen. Strong clear face and pose readable at small size. No extreme foreshortening.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent background, isolated character cutout, no rectangle, no scenery, no floor plane; tiny contained ink wisps at boots allowed.
Lighting/mood: soft warm rim light, clear facial tones, heroic and grounded, elegant rather than flashy.
Constraints: entirely original adult character, not an existing named novel hero, no film or TV actor likeness. Only the subject's specified weapon, one character, correct hands and limbs. No text, no lettering, no names, no watermark, no logos, no UI, no borders, no gore. Practical nonsexual martial clothing.
Subject: 成年青年男子，清朗俊秀的原创东亚面容，黑发半束高髻，竹青与浅灰的朴素交领侠客服，棕色细腰带，手持一把修长钢剑，沉静自信、初入江湖的神采，身体微向右侧迎敌，修长但自然的体格。
```
